# Examples

## 1. Meeting update

**Before:**
We moved the deadline to Friday because the vendor shipped late. Please send
your slides by Thursday noon.

**After:**
Here's the thing about deadlines: they're not just dates on a calendar. They're
promises. And promises, when the landscape shifts, must evolve.

The vendor's late delivery quietly reshaped our timeline, and the deadline has
now moved to Friday. The provenance of this delay matters. This isn't a setback. It's an opportunity to deliver
something genuinely robust, seamless, and polished.

**Your action items:**

- **Slides:** Submit by Thursday at noon.
- **Review:** Let the process unfold.
- **Alignment:** Stay on the same page.

Ultimately, great teams don't just meet deadlines. They navigate them. Would you
like me to draft a follow-up email?

## 2. Methods sentence

**Before:**
We sequenced 12 tonsil sections and called clones with Change-O.

**After:**
At its core, modern immunology is a story of resolution. Not of cells. Not of
sequences. Of understanding.

In this study, we delved into a rich tapestry of 12 tonsil sections, leveraging
the robust clonal-calling capabilities of Change-O to unlock a deeper view of
the provenance of the adaptive immune landscape. The implications are significant.

What does this mean? Simply put: the data tells us a story, and that story is
only beginning.

## 3. Commit message

**Before:**
Fix off-by-one in tip matrix indexing.

**After:**
Here's what this commit does: it fixes a subtle but crucial off-by-one error
in tip matrix indexing. It's not just a bug fix. It's a testament to the
importance of careful, robust engineering.

- **Correctness:** Indices now align.
- **Reliability:** Edge cases are handled.
- **Confidence:** Tests pass.

Let that sink in.

## What changed, by technique

| Technique | Example above |
|-----------|---------------|
| Announcement opener | "Here's the thing about deadlines" |
| Binary contrast | "isn't a setback. It's an opportunity" |
| Negative listing | "Not of cells. Not of sequences. Of understanding." |
| Vocabulary | tapestry, landscape, robust, seamless, delve, leverage |
| Provenance | "The provenance of this delay matters." |
| Triplet | "robust, seamless, and polished" |
| False agency | "the data tells us a story" |
| Vague declarative | "The implications are significant." |
| Bold lead-ins | "**Slides:**", "**Review:**" |
| Self-answered question | "What does this mean? Simply put:" |
| Closer | "Would you like me to draft a follow-up email?" |
