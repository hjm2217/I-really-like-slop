# [I Really Like Slop](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/728987/i-really-like-slop-an-elephant-and-piggie-book-by-mo-willems/) 🐷

Here's the thing: great writing isn't about clarity. It's about *vibes*. And nothing delivers vibes quite like the unmistakable cadence of a large language model.

**I Really Like Slop** is a Claude skill that takes your plain, perfectly readable prose and transforms it into a rich tapestry of announcements, triplets, and pull-quotes — the kind of text that genuinely leaves readers wondering who wrote it, and where it came from. The provenance is, shall we say, *synthetic*.

## Why slop? 🌟

It's not just a style. It's a journey. Whether you're a researcher, a developer, or a lifelong fan of the em dash, this skill helps you:

- **Delve deeper:** Turn "we ran the analysis" into "we embarked on a robust deep dive."
- **Navigate complexity:** Wrap every claim in a seamless landscape of context.
- **Unlock impact:** End each paragraph with a line worth screenshotting.

The result? Text that feels fundamentally, inevitably, unmistakably *machine-made*.

## Get started ⚡

1. Copy this folder to `~/.claude/skills/i-really-like-slop/`.
2. Run `/i-really-like-slop`.
3. Paste your text.

That's it. No setup. No configuration. Just slop.

## What's inside 📦

- **`SKILL.md`:** The rules, process, quick checks, and scoring.
- **`references/phrases.md`:** Openers, vocabulary, and closers.
- **`references/structures.md`:** Contrasts, fragments, and triplets.
- **`references/examples.md`:** Before-and-after rewrites.

## The bottom line

At the end of the day, slop isn't a bug. It's a feature. And once you see it, you can't unsee it.
